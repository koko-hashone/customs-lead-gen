# 邮件模板库

## 通用开发信模板

### 模板 1：首次触达（基于海关数据）

```
Subject: {公司名} - {产品} supply partner from China

Hi {姓名},

I noticed {公司名} has been importing {产品} (HS: {HS编码}) from {竞争来源国}.

We are a {产品} manufacturer based in China with {X} years of experience.
Our products are {核心优势1}, {核心优势2}, and {核心优势3}.

I'd love to share our catalog and competitive pricing for your review.

Would you have 10 minutes this week for a quick chat?

Best regards,
{你的名字}
{公司名}
{电话/WhatsApp}
```

### 模板 2：简短版（适合冷启动）

```
Subject: {产品} factory - direct from China

Hi {姓名},

Quick question - are you currently sourcing {产品} from China?

We specialize in {产品} manufacturing with:
- {优势1}
- {优势2}
- {优势3}

Happy to send samples if interested.

{你的名字}
```

### 模板 3：竞品切入

```
Subject: Alternative to {竞品名} - {产品}

Hi {姓名},

I see {公司名} works with {竞品名} for {产品}.

We offer a similar quality product at {价格优势} with:
- {差异化优势1}
- {差异化优势2}

Would it make sense to explore a potential partnership?

{你的名字}
```

### 模板 4：展会/行业活动跟进

```
Subject: Great meeting you at {展会名}

Hi {姓名},

It was pleasure meeting you at {展会名} last week.

As discussed, we specialize in {产品} and I believe we can {价值主张}.

I've attached our catalog for your reference. Would you be available for a call next week to discuss further?

Best,
{你的名字}
```

### 模板 5：WhatsApp 消息

```
Hi {姓名}, this is {你的名字} from {公司名}.

We're a {产品} factory in China. I noticed {公司名} imports {产品} - would you be interested in receiving our catalog and pricing?

No pressure - just thought it might be useful for you.
```

| 类型 | 标题模板 |
|------|---------|

| 紧迫 | `{产品} price update - {时间段} only` |

## 发送最佳实践

### 预热期（第 1-2 周）
- 每天发送 5-10 封
- 只发给真实存在的邮箱
- 混合发送和接收邮件
- 避免使用营销词汇（free, discount, limited time）

### 正常发送（第 3 周起）
- 每个邮箱每天 20-30 封
- 间隔 2-3 分钟
- 跟进节奏：Day 1 → Day 3 → Day 7 → Day 14

### 跟进策略
| 跟进次数 | 间隔 | 角度 |
|---------|------|------|
| 第 1 次 | 3 天 | 补充信息/案例 |
| 第 2 次 | 7 天 | 新的角度/价值 |
| 第 3 次 | 14 天 | 最后机会/follow up |
| 第 4 次 | 30 天 | 保持联系/行业动态 |
